Who we are

TippinOut is an income-tracking app operated by Jennifer Quinn, doing business as Subnivean Labs, in Ontario, Canada. Contact: support@tippinout.com.

Your work and income records

You choose whether to record workplaces, roles, clients, work hours, sales, tips, tipouts, commissions, other income, paychecks, schedules, notes and cashout photos. TippinOut stores those records and your app settings on your device to provide its tracking, calculations, history and reporting features. No TippinOut account is required.

Your work and income records and cashout photos are not uploaded to RevenueCat or Google AdMob by TippinOut. Subscription and advertising services handle separate information as described below. TippinOut does not provide cloud synchronization of your income records.

Backups, exports and photos

The app keeps rolling local safety snapshots in its own storage. You can make portable backups, restore selected backups and, where available, export reports. Android automatic portable backups save to a folder you select when enabled. Backups may contain records, settings and attached photos; reports contain the data selected for export.

You choose the save/share destination. If you use a cloud drive, email or another app, that provider handles the resulting file under its own practices. Backups and exports may contain sensitive financial or client information, so choose their destination carefully. Local snapshots are not a replacement for a backup saved outside the app.

Camera or photo access is used when you choose to attach a cashout photo. System file pickers let you select files for supported photo and backup features.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Subscriptions are processed through Google Play on Android or Apple's App Store on iOS. TippinOut uses RevenueCat to check access, present subscription options, process purchase/restore requests and manage subscription status.

RevenueCat processes a generated app-user identifier, purchase/transaction history and subscription status, along with technical information needed to operate its service, such as app/SDK versions and platform information. This generated identifier is not a TippinOut login. TippinOut does not ask you to give RevenueCat your income records, clients, photos, name or email as subscription attributes.

Payment details you enter in the store checkout are handled by the store and its payment providers. TippinOut does not receive your full payment-card details. Subscription information is sent to the relevant services over encrypted connections. RevenueCat also provides subscription reporting to help us understand purchases and operate the app.

See RevenueCat's privacy policy and the privacy information provided by Google or Apple.

Advertising and privacy choices

Free access may include banner ads and optional rewarded videos that provide temporary feature access. Active paid access removes these ads. Test versions may show demonstration ads.

TippinOut uses Google AdMob and Google's consent tools. When ads are requested, Google's advertising SDK may collect or share IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device/app identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate and improve its services, and detect fraud or abuse. Advertising-related information is transmitted over encrypted connections.

The current Android configuration blocks access to the Android advertising ID; this does not prevent all other identifiers or advertising-related data processing. TippinOut currently requests non-personalized ads, which can still involve data processing for measurement, security and delivery.

Where applicable, the app presents consent choices before ads can be requested. You can revisit available choices using the app's advertising privacy options. Choices and availability can vary by region. Changing an advertising choice does not delete your local income records or cancel a subscription.

See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Reminders and local access information

Optional shift, clock-out and payday reminders are scheduled locally. You can adjust reminder settings in the app and notification permission in your phone settings. Reminder text may appear on your lock screen according to your device settings.

The app stores local subscription-access checks, rewarded-unlock times and related access restrictions to apply its Free and paid features. Some device-specific access information is kept separately from portable backups so restoring a backup does not reset those restrictions.

TippinOut does not use Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. This does not mean the advertising and subscription services described above process no usage or performance information.

Support, retention and deletion

If you contact support, we receive the email address, message and any files you choose to send. We use these to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send payment-card details or an entire financial backup unless it is necessary and you choose to do so.

Local records remain on your device until you remove or replace them or the app's storage is removed. You can delete recorded entries and manage jobs/profiles within the available app controls. Restoring a backup replaces current local records after confirmation. Uninstalling may remove local records and snapshots, but does not remove files saved elsewhere or necessarily delete information already held by subscription/advertising providers.

Request data deletion

You can request deletion of information associated with TippinOut, operated by Subnivean Labs (Jennifer Quinn) without creating an account.

  1. Email support@tippinout.com with the subject TippinOut data deletion.
  2. Tell us whether your request concerns subscription information, advertising-related information, or support emails/attachments. Include Android or iOS and, if available, your RevenueCat app-user ID or a store transaction reference for subscription requests. If you cannot find an identifier, contact us first and we will help you locate the information needed.
  3. We will verify the request using only the information needed to locate your records, process deletion within our control and explain any provider steps or retention exceptions. Do not send your password, full payment-card details or an income backup.

What can be deleted and what may remain

  • RevenueCat subscription records: after verifying the relevant customer record, we can request deletion of the associated RevenueCat customer information, including its identifier, purchase history and subscription-status records. Google Play or Apple may separately retain store transaction records under their own policies. Continued use, purchases or restoration can create subscription records again.
  • Advertising-related information: we will assess the identifiers available and use applicable Google deletion processes for data associated with our app. Where the request requires Google's own privacy controls or another provider's process, we will explain the available steps. We cannot directly identify or erase every record held by Google or all advertising partners. Changing consent affects future processing and is not itself deletion of previously collected information.
  • Support correspondence: we can delete support emails and attachments held by us, subject to any necessary legal, security or dispute-related retention described below.
  • Income records, jobs, clients, schedules, photos and local access information: these are stored on your device; we cannot remotely erase them. Use the app's available deletion controls or remove the app's local storage. Removing local storage or uninstalling may permanently remove records and local safety snapshots. Exported reports and portable backups remain wherever you saved them until you delete those copies separately.

Retention exceptions

We do not impose a fixed additional retention period for records we can delete once a request is verified and any necessary processing is complete. If specific information must be kept to comply with a legal obligation or resolve a security issue or dispute, we will explain which information is retained, why, and the applicable retention period or the criteria used to determine it. We do not promise a single retention period for data independently held by Google, Apple or other providers; their policies and legal obligations apply.

Data deletion does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it separately through Google Play or the App Store to stop future renewals. We will not ask you to purchase a subscription to submit a deletion request.

Changes and contact

We update this notice when data practices change. The effective date will appear on the published policy. For privacy questions or requests, contact Subnivean Labs (Jennifer Quinn) at support@tippinout.com.
