Set up a job

Start in Jobs. Add your workplace and role, then choose how you record work and which wages, tips, sales, or commissions you want to track.

Log and review work

Use Add to record work for your selected job/profile. Calendar lets you review saved entries and schedules. Clock In tracks a shift as you work.

Detailed tutorial coming soon.

Understand paychecks and totals

Record the actual paycheck and its covered dates. Matched paychecks replace the earnings paid through payroll in your take-home total. Category splits are estimates; they are not an exact tax breakdown.

Detailed tutorial coming soon.

Keep a backup

Open Settings > Data & Backup to create a portable backup of your records and photos. Save it outside TippinOut. Restoring a backup replaces your current app data.

Detailed tutorial coming soon.

Free access and Pro

Free supports one active job/profile, offline logging, clock-in, and manual backups. Pro adds features including multiple active jobs, graphs, photos, and exports. Open Settings > Subscription for current plans, restore purchases, or manage your subscription.
